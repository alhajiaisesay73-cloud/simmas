About us

A Freetown aluminium and ceiling team you can rely on

For 4+ years, SIMMAS LTD has made aluminium windows, doors, kitchens and modern ceiling designs for homes, businesses and institutions across Sierra Leone.

Our story

Made here, fitted by us

SIMMAS LTD is run by Sheku Sesay from our workshop at 75 Wilkinson Road, Freetown. We started with one goal: aluminium and ceiling work that is done right and done on time.

Today we work for families building their homes, shop owners, offices, schools, churches and mosques, NGOs and government. Big job or small, every customer gets the same care.
